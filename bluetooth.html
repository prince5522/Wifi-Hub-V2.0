Compatibility and safety

  • Web Bluetooth works only in supported browsers and secure contexts such as HTTPS.
  • BLE service UUIDs and commands depend on the specific device; this tool cannot automatically control every device.
  • Many speakers and woofers use Bluetooth Classic audio, which this web interface cannot directly control. Use your phone’s Bluetooth settings for audio pairing.
  • Only connect to devices you own or have permission to use.
